Helen Macleod

My Practice

I am a calm and empathic person who likes to work alongside you, at a pace that suits you. I believe that a good therapeutic relationship is key to you feeling safe and able to share in a comfortable environment. I work with you to gain self-awareness and build resilience whilst learning skills and coping mechanisms to enable you to continue managing your own mental and emotional health after therapy has finished. One of the main beliefs I have within my practice is that you (the client) are the expert on you – I am here to help you understand and uncover the issues that have brought you to therapy, then help you process them in order to live your best life.

I mainly use CBT but will use other therapeutic methods, e.g. motivational interviewing and brief solution focused therapy, when needed.

You, the client, are the expert on you.

About Helen

I am a trained Cognitive Behavioural Therapist and have been practising CBT for the past 8 years. In addition to CBT I have over 25 years’ experience as a counsellor, mainly within the area of addiction, and more recently within mental health. Through my career I have worked in many different areas ranging across criminal justice and community settings. I have worked mainly with adults over 18, but have experience of working with 16–18 year olds.
