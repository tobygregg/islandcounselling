Victoria Gregg

My Practice

I offer a caring and collaborative service to individual teenagers and adults facing all sorts of life stresses and mental and emotional difficulties. This includes help for stress, anxiety and depression, grief and trauma, relationship issues, parenting struggles, substance abuse, OCD and phobias, and support with physical and mental health issues.

In addition to face to face and online counselling for individuals, I am also able to provide mental health and wellbeing education, group support, continuing professional development and debriefing. With an educational background in clinical psychology and counselling experience in a variety of different roles, I tailor my therapy to meet the specific needs of my clients. Whatever you are struggling with I will help you recognise underlying problems, process life difficulties, realise your strengths, challenge unhelpful thoughts and perhaps adjust certain behaviours to experience life in a more positive way.

No two people are the same, so my practice is integrative, drawing on counselling theories such as Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, Solution-focussed therapy, narrative and Gestalt therapy. I am person-centered in my approach and focus primarily on finding solutions to the presenting issue whilst also recognizing the influence of past events. I enjoy using creative approaches in counselling and aim to look at the whole person as we address troubling issues. My counselling room is a safe and confidential space to share and discuss things that you may find difficult to process with others.

About Victoria

Victoria has a background in clinical psychology and health education and experience in counselling children, teens, students, and adults across the lifespan.

In addition to individual counselling, Victoria has assisted with groups for attachment parenting, anger management and bereavement, led wellbeing groups and support groups and provided continuing professional education, peer support and critical incident debriefing.

She is an experienced parenting mentor and has worked as a children and youth worker plus as a trauma therapist for children who have suffered adverse childhood events and abuse. She has volunteered on a suicide hotline, on a camp for traumatized children and also worked as a hospital chaplain, helping patients, their relatives and staff members through times of crisis.
