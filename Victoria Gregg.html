Victoria Gregg

My Practice

I am an experienced counsellor with a medical background in clinical psychology as well as nursing. I enjoy working children, teenagers and adults and value having a holistic approach using evidence-based methods. I tailor my practice to each person, using an integrative approach informed by cognitive behaviour therapy (CBT), and using Gestalt and DBT techniques, plus various creative methods. I use a person-centred collaborative approach and acknowledging that my clients are the experts on their lives. I am comfortable working with people of all backgrounds, including those who are neurodiverse.

I offer both face-to-face and online counselling to those facing all sorts of issues including anxiety, depression, stress, trauma, bereavement, relationship struggles, emotional dysregulation and managing mental health challenges such as OCD and phobias. I can help with parenting concerns and can coach young people navigating their teenage years or a diagnosis of ADHD or autism. I am also specifically trained to provide emotional care to those facing the tragedy of baby loss, a bereavement through suicide and life-changing medical diagnoses. I can provide counsellor education and have been a moderator for level 2 and 3 CBCAB counselling courses.

About Victoria

I have a background in clinical psychology and health education and experience in counselling children, teens, students, and adults across the lifespan.

In addition to individual counselling, I have assisted with groups for attachment parenting, anger management and bereavement, led wellbeing groups and support groups and provided continuing professional education, peer support and critical incident debriefing.

I am an experienced parenting mentor and has worked as a children and youth worker plus as a trauma therapist for children who have suffered adverse childhood events and abuse. She has volunteered on a suicide hotline, on a camp for traumatized children and also worked as a hospital chaplain, helping patients, their relatives and staff members through times of crisis.

What to expect

I offer a warm, caring and confidential counselling service for young people and adults. Whether you are struggling today, wanting to heal from past hurts, or needing to problem solve for the future, I will partner with you to take you to a better place. I can help you recognise underlying problems, process life difficulties, recognise your strengths, challenge unhelpful thoughts or work alongside you, teaching coping skills to help you live life in a more positive way. Alternatively, my counselling room can be a safe, reflective space to share things that you may find difficult to discuss with others.
