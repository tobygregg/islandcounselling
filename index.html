About Us

Island Counselling Guernsey

Welcome to Island Counselling. We are a small group of Guernsey counsellors with a range of skills and expertise. Our aim is to provide a professional, reliable service that offers flexible and affordable counselling to the Guernsey community.

Location

We provide counselling from two different discreet sites on the island, one in L'Islet and one in St Martins with available parking.

Appointments

We typically offer face to face counselling and therapy but can provide online sessions on request.
